Cancel or manage your subscription

Cancel any time. No phone calls, no questions.

When you cancel, Miro keeps working until the end of the month you already paid for, then stops renewing. You won't be charged again.

Cancel or manage my subscription

Enter the email you subscribed with. Stripe emails you a sign-in link, and from there you can cancel, change your card, or download receipts.

Other ways to cancel

  • From Miro: Settings → License → Manage subscription. It opens the same page, already signed in.
  • From Link: Miro is sold through Link, so you can also cancel at app.link.com with your Link account.
  • Email us: write to mirogoonteam@protonmail.com from the email you subscribed with, and we'll cancel it for you.

Refunds

Cancelling stops future payments; the current month isn't refunded automatically. If something went wrong — you were charged by mistake, or Miro doesn't work on your PC — email us within 14 days of the charge and we'll sort it out. Where your local law gives you more rights (for example a cooling-off period), those apply.

After you cancel

  • Your license key stops working when the paid month ends. Miro then asks for a key again.
  • Your settings, history and memories stay on your PC. Subscribe again any time to pick up where you left off.
  • To free a PC for someone else instead, use Settings → License → Remove from this PC.
