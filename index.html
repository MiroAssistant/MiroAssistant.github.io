Stealth

Invisible when you share your screen.

Flip one switch and Miro's window is excluded from screen capture by Windows, so it stays out of shares and recordings in apps like Discord, Meet, Zoom, Teams and OBS. Needs Windows 10 (version 2004) or later — check before you share anything sensitive.

  • Kept out of screen shares and recordings
  • No taskbar button, no Alt+Tab entry
  • On or off from the tray, any time

Questions

Good to know.

What is Miro?

Miro is an AI assistant that sits at the top of your Windows screen like a dynamic island. Press a shortcut and ask: it can see your screen, listen, search the web, recap calls, set timers, remember things and do tasks on your PC.

Does Miro work on Mac, iPhone or Android?

Yes. The full island app is for Windows 10 and 11. Miro for phones runs on iPhone, iPad, Android and in a Mac's browser, and your subscription covers two PCs plus two phones or tablets.

How much does Miro cost?

$18.99 a month, billed through Stripe. There are no tiers and no usage caps from us, and you can cancel any time and keep Miro until the month you paid for runs out.

Do I need my own AI key?

Yes, a free Gemini or Groq API key. It takes about a minute to make, and Miro walks you through it on first run.

Can people on a call see Miro?

No. Miro's island and the boxes it draws are hidden from screenshots and screen shares, so only you see them.

Is my data private?

Your questions go straight from your device to the AI provider you pick, never through our servers. Keys are stored with Windows encryption, and Miro waits for your OK before running commands, typing or power actions.
