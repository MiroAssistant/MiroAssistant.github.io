Changelog

Miro updates itself while you're subscribed — you'll see a “Miro is here” window when a new version is ready.

0.8.2 October 14, 2026

  • Fish Audio is back — pick a Fish Audio voice in Settings to have answers read out in it. Windows voices still work as before.

0.8.1 October 14, 2026

  • Boxes land in the right place — when Miro points at something on your screen, the box now goes exactly around it.
  • Smoother launch animation — the glow around the startup card no longer gets cut off.
  • Removed the Fish Audio voice option. Spoken answers use your Windows voices.

0.8.0 October 13, 2026

  • Step-by-step plans. For bigger jobs — “apply to this job”, “make a chart from this table” — Miro shows its plan on the island with a progress bar and ticks each step off as it goes.
  • Miro points at things on your screen. When an answer is about something you can see — an error, a button, part of a chart — Miro draws a labelled box around it. You also get a quick scan when it reads your screen, and a label at the pointer when it uses your mouse. None of it shows up in screen shares.
  • Say “yes” to approve. When Miro asks before doing something, press the talk shortcut and say “yes” or “no”. If you asked by voice, it listens for your answer by itself.
  • “Allow for this task”. Approve once and let the rest of that job run without asking again. Approve buttons now say what they do — “Type it”, “Send”, “End it”.
  • Email a call recap to your team with one click.
  • A progress ring on running timers, and every answer shows which AI answered and how long it took.

0.7.2 October 12, 2026

  • Miro can use your mouse and keyboard. Ask it to do things in any app — “open Discord and tell Sam I'm on my way”, “turn on dark mode in this app”, “fill in this form”. It looks at the screen, moves the mouse, clicks, scrolls, drags and types, step by step, and checks each step worked. Clicks wait for your OK unless you've allowed everything in Settings → PC control. Needs Gemini as your AI.
  • Miro writes code for you. Open your editor and ask for a script or a change — Miro puts the code straight in where your cursor is, pasted in one go so the editor doesn't mess up the indentation. Whole files fit now (up to 20,000 characters).
  • Longer tasks: Miro can now take up to 25 steps in one go.
  • The user guide covers the new mouse and coding features.

0.7.1 October 11, 2026

  • Record a call with one click — a record button sits next to the mic. Click it again for the summary, decisions and action items. Ctrl+Shift+L still works.
  • User guide — every shortcut, every button and what to try, at the end of setup and any time in Settings → User guide or the tray menu.

0.7.0 October 11, 2026

  • Far fewer “rate limit” errors — when one AI model is busy, Miro switches to another with its own free quota, for answers and voice transcription. Call recaps miss less.
  • Secure updates — updates come straight from Miro's own server and are checked before they install.
  • Settings → AI explains how to lift your AI key's limits for good.

0.6.0 October 11, 2026

  • Automatic updates — new versions download in the background and install with one click. Your settings, keys and history are kept.
  • New checkout — subscriptions go through Stripe, and your license key is shown right after you pay. Manage or cancel any time from Settings → License.
