Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

This policy explains what information Miro collects, why, and who it goes to. "Miro", "we" and "us" mean the two independent developers who make Miro, operating from Canada (Miro isn't a registered company). It covers the Miro app for Windows, this website, and Miro's license server. Questions: mirogoonteam@protonmail.com.

The short version

  • We only see what we need to sell you a subscription: your email, your subscription status, your license key, and an anonymous ID for each PC you use Miro on (up to 2).
  • We never see what you ask Miro. Your questions, screenshots, voice, files and call recordings go straight from your PC to the AI provider you pick (Google Gemini or Groq), using your own API key.
  • Your settings, keys, history and memories stay on your PC. They are never sent to us.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe and Link. We never see your full card number.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling. We don't sell or rent your information, and Miro has no advertising or analytics.

What stays on your PC

Miro keeps the following only on your computer, in your Windows profile (%APPDATA%\Miro). We can't see any of it.

  • Settings — shortcuts, voice, appearance and other preferences.
  • API keys — your Gemini, Groq and Fish Audio keys, encrypted with your Windows account.
  • Your license — the key and its last known status, also encrypted with your Windows account.
  • Conversation history — if "Save history" is on. Saved without images or file contents.
  • Memories — facts you've asked Miro to remember.
  • Timers and reminders.
  • PC audio Miro keeps for "answer what I just heard" — if that feature is on, the last minute or so of your PC's sound is held in memory only, never saved to disk, and is only sent to your AI provider when you press the shortcut.
  • The "Hey Miro" wake word — runs fully offline on your PC; nothing you say is sent anywhere until Miro hears the wake word and you ask something.

Uninstalling Miro leaves this folder in place so a reinstall keeps your setup. To remove everything, delete %APPDATA%\Miro after uninstalling.

Free AI tiers may use your data. On free plans, a provider may keep and use what you send to improve its products, and people may review it. Google says this about the Gemini API's free tier at the time of writing. Turning on billing for your key usually changes that. Read your provider's terms before you send anything sensitive.

Call recaps. When you record a call, Miro captures your PC's sound and (if enabled) your microphone, sends the audio in chunks to your AI provider to transcribe, then asks it for a summary. The audio isn't saved on your PC or sent to us. You're responsible for telling the other people on the call and getting their consent where the law requires it.

Actions on your PC (opening apps, finding files, running commands, clicking, typing) run on your computer and aren't reported to anyone. When Miro uses the mouse for you, it takes screenshots of your screen as it goes and sends them to your AI provider so it can see where to click.

How we use it

We use the information we collect only to:

  • Run your subscription — take payment, check your license, deliver installers and updates, and let you manage or cancel. (Needed to provide what you bought.)
  • Stop misuse — enforce the 2-PC limit and keep installers for paying subscribers. (Our legitimate interest in protecting the product.)
  • Keep records the law requires — such as tax and accounting records of sales. (Legal obligation.)
  • Answer you — when you contact support.

We don't use your information for advertising, profiling or training AI, and we don't sell it.

How long we keep it, and how we protect it

Keeping it. Subscription and payment records stay with Stripe while you're subscribed, then as long as tax and accounting law requires (in Canada, generally six years). A PC ID is removed when you use "Remove from this PC". Our license server doesn't keep its own logs of your requests. Support emails are kept while we help you, then deleted within a year.

Protecting it.

  • Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • The secret keys our server uses (for Stripe, and for signing licenses and downloads) are stored encrypted with Cloudflare and never appear in the app or on the website.
  • Installers are never public. They're handed out only through signed links that expire, to paid-up subscribers.
  • On your PC, your API keys and license are encrypted with your Windows account.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your information safe and will tell affected users about a breach as the law requires.

Where it's processed. Our providers run servers in the United States and other countries, so your information may be processed outside your own country, under their safeguards.

Your choices and rights

  • See, fix or delete your information. Email mirogoonteam@protonmail.com and we'll answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example under Canada's PIPEDA, the EU/UK GDPR, or California's CCPA), you may also have the right to object to or limit how it's used, or to get a copy in a portable form.
  • Cancel any time from Miro (Settings → License → Manage subscription) or from your Link account.
  • Free up a PC with Settings → License → Remove from this PC.
  • Delete what's on your PC by deleting %APPDATA%\Miro, and clear history or memories any time in Settings.
  • Delete your Link data by asking Link. That also cancels any subscription bought through it.
  • Complain to your local privacy regulator (in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada) if you're unhappy with how we handled your request.

Children

Miro isn't meant for children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age), and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has subscribed, contact us and we'll delete their information.
